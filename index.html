Final Year
Thesis

Full-aircraft CFD under one-engine-inoperative loading

The thesis applies the Actuator Disk Method (ADMv2) to a complete ATR 72-600 wing-fuselage-nacelle-empennage geometry in ANSYS Fluent, coupled by one-way FSI to ANSYS Mechanical, to extract the asymmetric structural loads an OEI event puts through the airframe — at a computational cost that a university engineering department can actually afford.

  • MethodADMv2 actuator disk, k−ω SST turbulence closure, poly-hexcore mesh (616,523 elements)
  • Governing caseOne-engine-inoperative climb vs. symmetric take-off, full wing-fuselage-nacelle-empennage geometry
  • Headline result−6,752 Pa suction peak and 35.9 m²/s² TKE at the operative wing-nacelle junction
  • Efficiency~3 CPU hours vs. 170 (Yang et al., 2024) to 3,100 (Zhao et al., 2022) CPU hours in prior work
  • SupervisionCo-authored manuscript in preparation with Sqn Ldr S.O. Sanusi
